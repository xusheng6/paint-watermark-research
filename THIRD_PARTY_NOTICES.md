# Third party material and fixture provenance

The root MIT license applies to original research code and documentation. It does not relicense Microsoft's extracted data or third-party image content.

- `artifacts/trees.json`, `trees` entries in other model artifacts, and `artifacts/dct.bin` contain constants recovered from the Microsoft DLL identified by hash in the report. Underlying third-party rights remain with their respective owners. No Microsoft executable or library is redistributed.
- `artifacts/paint_cloud_sample.png` is a pre-existing research Paint canvas fixture, a simple line drawing. The historical name is retained for result compatibility; it is not evidence of cloud generation. `artifacts/texture*.png` derive from that canvas with seeded Gaussian noise and watermark transformations.
- `artifacts/noise*.png` and `gradient.png` are generated numerical fixtures. Modified versions are outputs of the tested DLL or the research implementation, as specified by their names and the report.

Microsoft, Paint, InvisMark, and other product names identify the subjects of independent research. This project is not endorsed by their owners.
